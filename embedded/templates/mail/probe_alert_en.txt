{{/*
  Delivery probe alert mail (English). Sent to the notification recipients
  once per probe mail when it becomes delayed and once when it becomes a
  failure or could not be sent (both groups in one mail). Fields:
    .Date     date and time the mail is built (recipient's time zone)
    .Failures entries of the failures and send failures
    .Delays   entries of the delayed probe mails
    .URL      the probe screen of MailCare
    .BaseURL  the public URL of MailCare
    .Admin    the recipient is an administrator (the error texts are for
              administrators only)
    .SendFailures  whether an entry is a send failure
    Every entry:
      .Address .Slot (send time, server time as in the subject) .Manual
      (sent by hand) .SentAt (date and time of sending, recipient's time
      zone, as .Date)
      .SendFailed (the SMTP server did not accept it) .Error (why;
      "" for recipients other than administrators)
      .Hours .Minutes (time without arrival)
*/ -}}
{{define "subject" -}}
[MailCare] Delivery probe
{{- if .Failures}} failure: {{len .Failures}}{{end}}
{{- if and .Failures .Delays}},{{end}}
{{- if .Delays}} delay: {{len .Delays}}{{end}} ({{.Date}})
{{- end}}

{{define "body" -}}
This is a notification from MailCare.
{{- if .Failures}}

{{len .Failures}} probe mail(s) did not arrive within the failure threshold or could not be sent (as of {{.Date}}).
Sending or receiving mail may be failing.
{{- range .Failures}}

* {{.Address}}
   Sent at: {{.SentAt}}{{if .Manual}} (Manual){{end}}
{{- if .SendFailed}}
   Status: Send failed
{{- if .Error}}
   Error: {{.Error}}
{{- end}}
{{- else}}
   Status: Failure (missing for {{.Hours}} h {{.Minutes}} min)
{{- end}}
{{- end}}
{{- if and .SendFailures (not .Admin)}}

Details of send errors are shown to administrators.
{{- end}}
{{- end}}
{{- if .Delays}}

{{len .Delays}} probe mail(s) did not arrive within the delay threshold (as of {{.Date}}).
If they do not arrive within the failure threshold, another mail reports them as failures.
{{- range .Delays}}

* {{.Address}}
   Sent at: {{.SentAt}}{{if .Manual}} (Manual){{end}}
   Status: Delayed (missing for {{.Hours}} h {{.Minutes}} min)
{{- end}}
{{- end}}

Check the status and mark the probe mails as resolved at:
{{.URL}}

--------------------------------------------------
This mail was sent automatically by MailCare.
Notification settings: {{.BaseURL}}/settings/notifications
{{- end}}
